About Me

I am a Liberian, who is interested in studying Applied Technology with focus on Software Development. My interest grows because I see technology as step forward for development in developing countries, like my country Liberia. As someone who loves teaching, I can use my skill acquired to help others learn technology to become self-reliant.

Additionally, I studied agriculture and environment. I see technology-software development as a mean to integrate the others and make people life better. Importantly, I am a member of the LDS, serving as Second Councilor in my ward. I have five children with my wife Etta Doe-Peters. My family and I like going out and travel locally. I hope to improve and help others become better.
